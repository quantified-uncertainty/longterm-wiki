Deployment methods focus on maintaining safety during AI system operation.

**Containment:**
- **<EntityLink id="E485" name="sandboxing">Sandboxing</EntityLink>**: Isolating AI systems from the outside world
- **<EntityLink id="E6" name="ai-control">AI Control</EntityLink>**: Maintaining human oversight and control

**Access Management:**
- **<EntityLink id="E486" name="structured-access">Structured Access</EntityLink>**: Tiered access to model capabilities
- **<EntityLink id="E487" name="tool-restrictions">Tool Restrictions</EntityLink>**: Limiting available actions and tools

**Output Safety:**
- **<EntityLink id="E595" name="output-filtering">Output Filtering</EntityLink>**: Screening model outputs for harm

**Multi-System:**
- **<EntityLink id="E488" name="multi-agent">Multi-Agent Safety</EntityLink>**: Safety in systems with multiple AI agents
