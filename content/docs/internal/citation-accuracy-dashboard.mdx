Per-citation accuracy scores from LLM claim-vs-source verification. Shows whether the text at each cited URL actually supports the claim made in the wiki page. Flag pages with low accuracy for manual review or run `pnpm crux w citations fix-inaccuracies --apply` to auto-fix. Scores below 0.5 indicate the citation likely doesn't support the claim.
